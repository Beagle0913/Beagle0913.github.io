Asveja Twisties Loop

Vilnius → Kernavė → Širvintos → Dubingiai → Molėtai → Labanoras → Pabradė → Vilnius

A clockwise loop built around the two best-rated corner roads in Lithuania, Dubingiai–Bijutiškis and on into Molėtai, with Labanoras forest roads on the way back. Stops are fuel, lunch and a couple of five-minute views.

Before you go

  • Road 173 (Molėtai–Pabradė) is being rebuilt in sections through 2027–2028. Where work is active, one lane is closed and traffic alternates, so expect short waits.
  • The Pabradė area has a large military training ground. Stay on public roads and give convoys room.
  • The Dubingiai road has blind corners on narrow tarmac. Riders who rated it say to keep your speed down.
  • Fuel at Širvintos (km 70) and Molėtai (km 130) covers the whole loop on a full tank.
  • Want it longer? Carry on from Labanoras toward Kaltanėnai and Švenčionėliai before turning south to Pabradė. That adds about 35 km.
