Vilnius Moto Routes

Rides that start and end in Vilnius. Each route has a road book with times and road surfaces, a GPX for Kurviger, and buttons for Google Maps and Waze. Once you open a route, use the bar at the top to switch between routes.

Distances and times come from OpenStreetMap car routing, and surfaces are partly estimated. Check the route in Kurviger before you ride.
