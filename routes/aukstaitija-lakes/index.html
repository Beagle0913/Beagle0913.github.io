Load the route

You have two GPX files (one per day) and a KML with every stop. Kurviger is the one to use for the gravel; Google Maps and Waze will send you down the fastest roads between stops.

Best for mixed terrain

Kurviger

Import the day's GPX (website: menu → Import → file; app: open the file from your phone's Files and share it to Kurviger). Kurviger turns the points into waypoints and calculates its own route. Pick a curvy profile and check the avoidances so unpaved roads are not excluded. Change the Vilnius start to your own address.

Fastest setup

Google Maps

Each day has buttons below that open Google Maps with every stop filled in. Google caps the number of stops per route, so Day 1 is split into two parts at Palūšė. Expect Google to prefer asphalt.

One leg at a time

Waze

Waze can't import a multi-stop route. Tap the Waze button on the next stop each time you set off.

See it all on one map

Google My Maps

Import the KML at mymaps.google.com to get both days as layers. It then shows up in the Google Maps app under Saved → Maps.

Camping instead

Tents are only allowed at designated campsites. The national park has campsites around Palūšė and the lakes, but some close for the season. Ask at the Palūšė visitor centre (you pass it around 12:35) which ones are open and what the fee is. Plan for a night near 5 °C.

Before you go

  • Forecast as of Wednesday: Saturday dry, up to 19 °C but around 5 °C at the 08:30 start. Sunday around 16 °C with a small chance of rain. Check again on Friday.
  • Park gravel roads carry walkers, cyclists and cars going to the lakes. Keep speeds low around blind crests on the Šiliniškės ridge.
  • Autumn weekends are hunting season. Watch for hunters' cars parked on forest roads and for deer at dusk.
  • Mobile signal can drop in the forests. Download offline maps for the area in Kurviger or Google Maps before you leave.
  • Times assume car speeds plus the stops shown. On gravel you will be slower, which the hour of buffer before sunset is there to cover.

Distances and ride times are from OpenStreetMap car routing between the stops; your Kurviger route will differ a little. Opening hours are from the places' own listings and can change.
